\begin{abstract}
When a desktop or laptop operating system tethers to a smartphone Personal Hotspot (5G/LTE over Wi-Fi or USB), six interacting systems pathologies degrade interactive latency: (1) multi-megabyte host TCP socket buffers saturate un-managed smartphone baseband queues, inducing 450--1,200+~ms bufferbloat stalls; (2) symmetric ICMP Echo RTT controllers conflate uplink and downlink congestion, falsely throttling download capacity during heavy uploads; (3) fixed-step multiplicative-decrease (AIMD) controllers require 5--6 seconds to track abrupt cellular fades; (4) naive Gaussian or plug-in Pareto estimators underestimate heavy-tailed 5G/LTE scheduling spikes due to finite-sample Maximum Likelihood bias; (5) periodic Apple Wireless Direct Link (\texttt{awdl0}) social-channel scans induce 120~ms Wi-Fi radio stutters; and (6) cascading GTP-U, RFC~7335 CLAT46 (\texttt{464XLAT}), and overlay VPN (\texttt{utun*}) headers trigger silent Path MTU blackholes.

We present \textbf{Mirabilis-Tether} (\texttt{tether-optimize}), a zero-dependency macOS/XNU kernel optimization engine and native menu bar suite whose entire mathematical and state-transition specification is machine-checked in \textbf{110 zero-axiom constructive Lean~4 theorems} (\texttt{TetherOptimizeSpec.lean}, 4,099 lines, zero external imports) and validated at runtime by \textbf{72 live system assertions}. Our mathematical framework unifies: (i) \emph{Mirabilis Abbreviatio}, an exact small-sample debiasing of the Hill--Taleb Pareto tail estimator $\widehat{\alpha}_{\mathrm{MLE}} \sim \mathrm{InvGamma}(n, n\alpha)$ paired with the Cirillo--Taleb compact-support Shadow Mean; (ii) a discrete formulation of \textbf{Pontryagin's Maximum Principle} over logarithmic Kelly utility solved in $O(1)$ integer arithmetic via the floor-division Galois connection; (iii) a \textbf{Revealed-Capacity Costate Dual} driven by 64-bit \texttt{NET\_RT\_IFLIST2} kernel counters that captures abrupt $100 \to 12$~Mbps cellular fades in a single 500~ms step ($11\times$ faster than AIMD); and (iv) a parallel 3-reflector BSD \texttt{IPPROTO\_ICMP} \textbf{RFC~792 Type~13/14 One-Way Delay quorum} that algebraically cancels arbitrary host-reflector clock offsets $\theta \in \mathbb{Z}$ while rejecting single-reflector spikes via Birkhoff's distributive median. Live evaluation on Apple Silicon (M1) demonstrates a $697\times$ counter-sensing speedup ($7.8~\mu\text{s}$ vs.\ $5.41~\text{ms}$), $0.0\%$ standby CPU overhead, exact wire-level $\theta$-cancellation, and zero packet fragmentation or retransmissions.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Modern cellular networks (5G NR and LTE-Advanced) exhibit extreme non-stationarity: air-interface capacity fluctuates by an order of magnitude within hundreds of milliseconds due to carrier aggregation switches, millimeter-wave/mid-band handovers, RLC retransmissions, and physical shadowing. When a macOS host tethers to an iPhone, iPad, or Android device via Wi-Fi (\texttt{en0}) or USB (\texttt{en7}/\texttt{en17}), the host kernel treats the link as a stationary high-bandwidth Ethernet or WLAN interface.

Specifically, six structural mismatches arise between the host networking stack and the mobile hotspot:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
  \item \textbf{Baseband Bufferbloat}: Stock macOS permits TCP socket buffers to auto-scale up to $16\text{ MiB}$. On an $8\text{ Mbps}$ ($1,000\text{ B/ms}$) cellular uplink, a single bulk flow can enqueue $16,777,216\text{ B}$ into the smartphone's baseband FIFO, creating up to $16.7\text{ s}$ of queueing delay behind which interactive DNS, SSH, and WebRTC packets stall.
  \item \textbf{Symmetric RTT Blindness}: Existing user-space autorate tools probe the link with symmetric ICMP Echo (\texttt{Type 8/0}) requests. Because $\mathrm{RTT} = d_\uparrow + d_\downarrow$, a pure upload saturation inflates RTT and causes symmetric controllers to slash the \emph{download} shaper pipe by $50\text{--}80\%$, starving downlink throughput even when the cellular downlink is completely idle.
  \item \textbf{Multi-Second Lag on Abrupt Fades}: Classical AIMD autorate loops decay the shaper ceiling multiplicatively ($r_{k+1} = \lfloor 0.8 r_k \rfloor$). If a user enters an elevator and cellular capacity drops from $100\text{ Mbps}$ to $12\text{ Mbps}$, multiplicative decay requires $11$ consecutive bloated ticks ($5.5\text{ s}$ at $500\text{ ms}$/tick) to converge, during which the baseband buffer overflows continuously.
  \item \textbf{Fat-Tailed Scheduling Spikes and Small-Sample MLE Bias}: Cellular MAC scheduling delays follow power-law Pareto/Lomax distributions $P(X > x) = (x/L)^{-\alpha}$ rather than Gaussian tails. In small rolling windows ($n \in [4, 20]$), the Maximum Likelihood (Hill) estimator $\widehat{\alpha}_{\mathrm{MLE}} = n / \sum_{i=1}^n \ln(X_i / L)$ is Inverse-Gamma distributed with expectation $\mathbb{E}[\widehat{\alpha}_{\mathrm{MLE}}] = \frac{n}{n-1}\alpha > \alpha$, systematically overestimating tail thinness and underestimating spike risk.
  \item \textbf{AWDL Social-Channel Radio Stutters}: On macOS, the Apple Wireless Direct Link (\texttt{awdl0}) interface periodically hops off the active Wi-Fi AP channel to scan social channels (AirDrop/Handoff), introducing periodic $80\text{--}150\text{ ms}$ latency spikes on Wi-Fi hotspots.
  \item \textbf{Cascading Encapsulation Blackholes}: Carrier GTP-U tunnels and RFC~7335 CLAT46 (\texttt{464XLAT}) IPv4-over-IPv6 translation reduce effective path MTU to $1420\text{--}1450\text{ B}$. When combined with overlay VPNs (Tailscale/WireGuard \texttt{utun*} adding $80\text{ B}$), un-scrubbed TCP segments suffer silent fragmentation drops.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Algebraic \& Lattice Foundations of Network State Control}
\label{sec:lattice}

All proofs in \texttt{TetherOptimizeSpec.lean} are constructed from first principles over inductive Peano naturals and integers so that every theorem reduces purely in the Lean~4 kernel without axioms.

\subsection{Bounded Join-Semilattice Catamorphism \& Interface Partition}

Let $(L, \sqcup, \bot, \top)$ be a bounded join-semilattice of network operating modes (ordered from passive Home LAN/Wi-Fi $\bot$ through constrained Wi-Fi hotspot to active USB Personal Hotspot $\top$). Interface classification partitions hardware ports into three mutually disjoint sets:
\[
  \mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{hotspot}} \cap \mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{protected}} = \emptyset, \qquad
  \mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{overlay}} \cap \mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{hotspot}} = \emptyset,
\]
where $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{hotspot}} = \{\texttt{en0}_{\mathrm{tether}}, \texttt{en7}, \texttt{en17}\}$ covers iOS (`172.20.10.0/28`), Android (`192.168.43.0/24`, `192.168.49.0/24`), and RFC~7335 CLAT46 (`192.0.0.0/29`), while $\mathcal{P}_{\mathrm{protected}} = \{\texttt{en8}, \texttt{en9}, \texttt{en12}, \texttt{en13}, \texttt{en22}\}$ protects downstream Ethernet bridges and VMware USB passthrough NICs (\textbf{Theorems 1--12}).

\begin{theorem}[Semilattice Catamorphism Uniqueness --- Theorem 91]
Let $f : A \to L$ map each active interface observation to a bounded join-semilattice $(L, \sqcup, \bot)$ with absorbing top $\top$. The fold catamorphism $\Phi_f([a_1, \dots, a_k]) = f(a_1) \sqcup \dots \sqcup f(a_k) \sqcup \bot$ is a monoid homomorphism under list concatenation, $\Phi_f(xs \mathbin{+\!+} ys) = \Phi_f(xs) \sqcup \Phi_f(ys)$, is idempotent on duplicate observations, and short-circuits identically upon encountering $\top$.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Encapsulation Galois Adjunction \& Dual PF Scrubbing}

Consider a packet with inner TCP payload $p \in \mathbb{N}$ traversing an encapsulation stack of total header overhead $H \in \mathbb{N}$ over a link of Maximum Transmission Unit $M \in \mathbb{N}$. Define the monotone maps:
\[
  \mathrm{encap}_H(p) = p + H, \qquad \mathrm{decap}_H(M) = M \ominus H = \max(0, M - H).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Encapsulation Galois Connection --- Theorem 93]
For all $p, H, M \in \mathbb{N}$, $(\mathrm{encap}_H, \mathrm{decap}_H)$ forms a monotone Galois connection $\mathrm{encap}_H \dashv \mathrm{decap}_H$:
\[
  \mathrm{encap}_H(p) \le M \iff p \le \mathrm{decap}_H(M).
\]
Consequently, for compound encapsulation (inner TCP/IPv6 $H_1 = 60\text{ B}$ inside WireGuard/Tailscale \texttt{utun*} $H_2 = 80\text{ B}$ over cellular MTU $M = 1420\text{ B}$), the maximum non-fragmenting inner MSS is the composition of right adjoints:
\[
  \mathrm{MSS}_{\mathrm{inner}} \le \mathrm{decap}_{H_1}(\mathrm{decap}_{H_2}(M)) = M \ominus (H_1 + H_2) = 1420 - 140 = 1280\text{ B},
\]
and clamping $\mathrm{MSS}_{\mathrm{outer}} = 1380\text{ B}$ and $\mathrm{MSS}_{\mathrm{overlay}} = 1220\text{ B}$ via idempotent PF \texttt{max-mss} scrubbing ($\min(p, c_1, c_2) = \min(p, \min(c_1, c_2))$, \textbf{Theorem 94}) strictly prevents L3 fragmentation across all IPv4, IPv6, GTP-U, and CLAT46 paths (\textbf{Theorems 13--28}).
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Jacobson--Karels Adaptive Deadband Invariant}

To prevent chattering under baseline wireless jitter, let $v \in \mathbb{N}$ denote the RFC~6298 EWMA mean deviation ($\mathrm{RTTVAR}$). We define the adaptive hysteresis thresholds:
\[
  \Theta_{\mathrm{high}}(v) = 30 + \min(60, 2v), \qquad \Theta_{\mathrm{low}}(v) = 12 + \min(20, \lfloor v/2 \rfloor).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Uniform Deadband Separation --- Theorem 95]
For all $v \in \mathbb{N}$, the deadband width satisfies $\Theta_{\mathrm{high}}(v) - \Theta_{\mathrm{low}}(v) \ge 18\text{ ms}$ and is monotonically non-decreasing in $v$. Thus the bloated ($\Delta q > \Theta_{\mathrm{high}}(v)$) and drained ($\Delta q \le \Theta_{\mathrm{low}}(v)$) decision regions are strictly separated for all $v \ge 0$.
\end{theorem}

\section{\emph{Mirabilis Abbreviatio}: Inverse-Gamma Fat-Tail Estimation \& Shadow Means}
\label{sec:taleb}

Cellular queueing spikes above a baseline threshold $L > 0$ follow a Pareto Type~I tail:
\[
  f(x \mid \alpha, L) = \frac{\alpha L^\alpha}{x^{\alpha + 1}}, \quad x \ge L, \quad \alpha > 0.
\]
Given $n$ exceedance observations $X_1, \dots, X_n \ge L$, the sufficient statistic $S_n = \sum_{i=1}^n \ln(X_i / L)$ satisfies $S_n \sim \mathrm{Gamma}(n, \alpha)$ with density $g(s) = \frac{\alpha^n}{\Gamma(n)} s^{n-1} e^{-\alpha s}$.

\subsection{Exact Small-Sample Bias of the Hill MLE and MVUE Debiasing}

The Maximum Likelihood (Hill) estimator is $\widehat{\alpha}_{\mathrm{MLE}} = \frac{n}{S_n}$. Since $S_n \sim \mathrm{Gamma}(n, \alpha)$, its reciprocal follows an Inverse-Gamma distribution:
\[
  \widehat{\alpha}_{\mathrm{MLE}} = \frac{n}{S_n} \sim \mathrm{InvGamma}(\text{shape}=n,\, \text{scale}=n\alpha).
\]
For $n > 1$, the first moment of $\mathrm{InvGamma}(n, \beta)$ is $\frac{\beta}{n-1}$, which yields:
\[
  \mathbb{E}[\widehat{\alpha}_{\mathrm{MLE}}] = \frac{n\alpha}{n - 1} = \left(1 + \frac{1}{n-1}\right)\alpha > \alpha.
\]
In small real-time windows (e.g.\ $n = 4$), raw MLE overestimates $\alpha$ by $+33.3\%$, creating a dangerous illusion of thin tails (\emph{Taleb's small-sample fat-tail trap}). Multiplying by the Bessel-like factor $\frac{n-1}{n}$ yields the exact Minimum Variance Unbiased Estimator (MVUE):
\[
  \widehat{\alpha}_0 = \frac{n - 1}{S_n} = \frac{n - 1}{n}\widehat{\alpha}_{\mathrm{MLE}}, \qquad \mathbb{E}[\widehat{\alpha}_0] = \alpha \quad (\forall n \ge 2).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Inverse-Gamma Small-Sample Debiasing --- Theorems 96 \& 97]
For all integer sample sizes $n \ge 2$ and positive log-exceedance sums $S_n > 0$, the debiased estimator $\widehat{\alpha}_0(n, S_n) = \lfloor (n-1)/S_n \rfloor$ satisfies $(n-1)\widehat{\alpha}_{\mathrm{MLE}} = n\widehat{\alpha}_0$ in exact scaled arithmetic, strictly bounds $\widehat{\alpha}_0 < \widehat{\alpha}_{\mathrm{MLE}}$, and converges monotonically toward $\widehat{\alpha}_{\mathrm{MLE}}$ as $n \to \infty$.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Posterior Predictive Lomax Tail \& Cirillo--Taleb Shadow Mean}

Integrating out the uncertain parameter $\alpha$ against the $\mathrm{Gamma}(n, S_n)$ likelihood yields the exact Bayesian posterior predictive survival function (a Lomax / Pareto Type~II distribution):
\[
  P(X_{\mathrm{new}} > x \mid S_n, n) = \int_0^\infty \left(\frac{x}{L}\right)^{-\alpha} \frac{S_n^n}{\Gamma(n)} \alpha^{n-1} e^{-\alpha S_n}\,d\alpha = \left(1 + \frac{\ln(x/L)}{S_n}\right)^{-n},
\]
with exact $p$-quantile $\mathrm{VaR}_p^\star = L \exp\!\left(S_n \big((1-p)^{-1/n} - 1\big)\right)$ (\textbf{Theorem 99}).

When $\widehat{\alpha}_0 \le 1$ (the Cauchy/L\'evy infinite-mean regime), the unconditional expectation $\mathbb{E}[X] = \infty$, but physical cellular timeouts truncate observations at $H = 350\text{ ms}$. Following Cirillo and Taleb, we compute the dual-bounded \textbf{Shadow Mean} on $[L, H]$:
\[
  \mu_{\mathrm{shadow}}(\alpha, L, H) =
  \begin{cases}
    \dfrac{\alpha L}{\alpha - 1}\,\dfrac{1 - (L/H)^{\alpha - 1}}{1 - (L/H)^\alpha}, & \alpha \ne 1, \\[10pt]
    \dfrac{L \ln(H/L)}{1 - L/H}, & \alpha = 1,
  \end{cases}
\]
which is strictly finite, continuous across $\alpha = 1$, and strictly exceeds the naive sample mean throughout the heavy-tailed regime $\alpha \in (0, 2)$ (\textbf{Theorem 98}).

\section{Pontryagin's Maximum Principle \& Galois Control Duality}
\label{sec:pontryagin}

Rather than tuning heuristic AIMD step sizes, we cast cellular rate control as a discrete-time optimal control problem via \textbf{Pontryagin's Maximum Principle (PMP)}.

\subsection{The Discrete Kelly--Pontryagin Hamiltonian}

Let $q_k \in \mathbb{N}$ be the baseband queue state, $u_k \in [u_{\min}, u_{\max}] \subset \mathbb{N}_+$ be the dummynet shaper control rate (in Mbps), $\tau_0 = 20\text{ ms}$ be the target queue delay, and $W = u_{\max}\tau_0$ be the target Bandwidth-Delay Product scale. Define the discrete Kelly logarithmic utility:
\[
  U_W(u) = \sum_{j=1}^u \left\lfloor \frac{W}{j} \right\rfloor, \qquad \Delta U_W(u) = U_W(u) - U_W(u-1) = \left\lfloor \frac{W}{u} \right\rfloor.
\]
With discrete queue dynamics $q_{k+1} = \max(0, q_k + u_k - c_k)$ and positive shadow price (costate) $\lambda_k \ge 1$ (in ms of marginal congestion cost per Mbps), the discrete Pontryagin Hamiltonian is:
\[
  \mathcal{H}(q_k, u, -\lambda_k) = U_W(u) + \lambda_k (u_{\max} - u).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Global Maximization of the Pontryagin Hamiltonian --- Theorem 101]
For every $u_{\min} \le u_{\max}$, $W \in \mathbb{N}$, and shadow price $\lambda \ge 1$, the closed-form integer control law
\[
  u^\star(\lambda) = \mathrm{clamp}_{[u_{\min}, u_{\max}]}\!\left(\left\lfloor \frac{W}{\lambda} \right\rfloor\right)
\]
globally maximizes $\mathcal{H}(q, u, -\lambda)$ over all $u \in [u_{\min}, u_{\max}]$:
\[
  \forall u \in [u_{\min}, u_{\max}], \qquad \mathcal{H}(q, u^\star(\lambda), -\lambda) \ge \mathcal{H}(q, u, -\lambda).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof Sketch (Formalized in Lean~4 lines 3130--3340)]
The forward difference of the Hamiltonian at step $u$ is $\mathcal{H}(u) - \mathcal{H}(u-1) = \lfloor W/u \rfloor - \lambda$. By the fundamental Galois connection of integer floor division (\texttt{peanoDiv\_galois}: $q \cdot d \le W \iff q \le \lfloor W/d \rfloor$) combined with commutativity of multiplication,
\[
  \left\lfloor \frac{W}{u} \right\rfloor \ge \lambda \iff \lambda \cdot u \le W \iff u \cdot \lambda \le W \iff u \le \left\lfloor \frac{W}{\lambda} \right\rfloor.
\]
Thus $\mathcal{H}(q, u, -\lambda)$ is strictly non-decreasing for all $u \le \lfloor W/\lambda \rfloor$ and strictly decreasing for all $u > \lfloor W/\lambda \rfloor$. Clamping $\lfloor W/\lambda \rfloor$ to the convex interval $[u_{\min}, u_{\max}]$ therefore yields the exact global maximum on $[u_{\min}, u_{\max}]$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Taleb-Forced Adjoint Equation \& Lyapunov Costate Relaxation}

The costate $\lambda_k$ is driven by the \textbf{Taleb-Forced Adjoint Target} (\textbf{Theorem 104}):
\[
  \Psi(\tau_0, \Delta q_k, \widehat{\alpha}_0, \mu_{\mathrm{shadow}}) = \tau_0 + \Delta q_k + \min\!\left(120,\; \left\lfloor \frac{\max(0, 2000 - 1000\widehat{\alpha}_0)\,\mu_{\mathrm{shadow}}}{2000} \right\rfloor\right),
\]
which vanishes the tail risk premium when $\widehat{\alpha}_0 \ge 2$ (finite variance) and amplifies the shadow price proportionally to the Cirillo--Taleb Shadow Mean when $\widehat{\alpha}_0 < 2$. When the queue drains, $\lambda_k$ relaxes toward $\tau_0$ via $\lambda_{k+1} = \tau_0 + \lfloor 3(\lambda_k - \tau_0)/4 \rfloor$, which is a strict discrete Lyapunov contraction on $V(\lambda) = \lambda - \tau_0$ (\textbf{Theorem 105}).

\section{Closing the Real-World Gap: OWD Quorum \& Revealed-Capacity Duality}
\label{sec:sota}

\subsection{RFC 792 One-Way Delay Clock-Offset Cancellation}

Let $\theta \in \mathbb{Z}$ be the unknown clock offset (plus any sub-day timezone discrepancy) between the macOS host's UTC clock and a remote ICMP reflector's UTC midnight clock. An RFC~792 Type~13/14 Timestamp exchange records four timestamps $(T_1, T_2, T_3, T_4)$, producing apparent one-way delays:
\[
  \tilde{d}_\uparrow = T_2 - T_1 = b_\uparrow + \Delta q_\uparrow + \theta, \qquad
  \tilde{d}_\downarrow = T_4 - T_3 = b_\downarrow + \Delta q_\downarrow - \theta,
\]
and apparent minimum baselines $\tilde{b}_\uparrow = b_\uparrow + \theta$, $\tilde{b}_\downarrow = b_\downarrow - \theta$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact Clock-Offset Cancellation \& Conservation --- Theorem 106]
For all $b_\uparrow, \Delta q_\uparrow, b_\downarrow, \Delta q_\downarrow \in \mathbb{N}$ and all clock offsets $\theta \in \mathbb{Z}$:
\[
  \tilde{d}_\uparrow - \tilde{b}_\uparrow = \Delta q_\uparrow, \qquad
  \tilde{d}_\downarrow - \tilde{b}_\downarrow = \Delta q_\downarrow, \qquad
  \tilde{d}_\uparrow + \tilde{d}_\downarrow = (b_\uparrow + \Delta q_\uparrow) + (b_\downarrow + \Delta q_\downarrow) = \mathrm{RTT}.
\]
\end{theorem}
Moreover, whenever the link is in an empty-queue state ($\mathrm{RTT} \le \mathrm{baseRTT} + 4\text{ ms}$), \texttt{tether-events} re-anchors $(\tilde{b}_\uparrow, \tilde{b}_\downarrow)$ so continuous quartz crystal clock skew ($\approx 10\text{--}50\text{ ppm}$) never accumulates into false one-way bloat.

\subsection{Byzantine 2-of-3 Multi-Reflector Quorum}

To eliminate false throttling from single-reflector ICMP rate-limiting, \texttt{tether-events} probes three diverse anycast reflectors in parallel over non-privileged BSD \texttt{SOCK\_DGRAM} \texttt{IPPROTO\_ICMP} sockets (\texttt{9.9.9.9} Type~13, \texttt{149.112.112.112} Type~13, and \texttt{1.1.1.1} Type~8) and aggregates RTTs via Birkhoff's distributive lattice median:
\[
  \mathrm{median3}(a, b, c) = \max(\min(a, b),\, \min(\max(a, b), c)).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Byzantine Single-Reflector Glitch Immunity --- Theorem 107]
If two reflectors report true delays $r_1, r_2 \in [L, H]$ and any one reflector suffers an arbitrary Byzantine spike $B \ge H$, then $\mathrm{median3}$ lies strictly in $[L, H]$ across all $3! = 6$ permutations of $(r_1, r_2, B)$.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Revealed-Capacity Costate Dual: 1-Step Cellular Fade Capture}

Suppose the shaper ceiling is $u_{\max} = 100\text{ Mbps}$ ($W = u_{\max}\tau_0 = 2000$), and the user steps into an elevator where actual cellular delivery drops to $\hat{r} = 12\text{ Mbps}$ (measured over $500\text{ ms}$ via 64-bit \texttt{NET\_RT\_IFLIST2} counters) while bloat triggers.

A naive inversion $\lambda_{\mathrm{naive}} = \lfloor W / \hat{r} \rfloor$ fails because floor division rounds down, so $\lfloor W / \lfloor W / \hat{r} \rfloor \rfloor$ can exceed $\hat{r}$ (for $W = 100, \hat{r} = 30$, $\lfloor 100/30 \rfloor = 3 \implies \lfloor 100/3 \rfloor = 33 > 30$). Instead, we define the \textbf{Revealed-Capacity Costate Dual}:
\[
  \lambda_{\mathrm{cap}}(W, \hat{r}) = \left\lfloor \frac{W}{\hat{r} + 1} \right\rfloor + 1.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Revealed-Capacity Galois Dual \& 1-Step Fade Capture --- Theorem 108]
For all $W, \hat{r} \in \mathbb{N}$:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
  \item $\lambda_{\mathrm{cap}}(W, \hat{r}) \ge 1$ (never divides by zero, even if $\hat{r} = 0$);
  \item $\left\lfloor \dfrac{W}{\lambda_{\mathrm{cap}}(W, \hat{r})} \right\rfloor \le \hat{r}$ (the induced Pontryagin rate never exceeds revealed capacity $\hat{r}$);
  \item For $W = 2000$ and an elevator fade $\hat{r} = 12\text{ Mbps}$, $\lambda_{\mathrm{cap}}(2000, 12) = 154\text{ ms}$ and $u^\star(154) = \lfloor 2000/154 \rfloor = 12\text{ Mbps}$ in a \textbf{single 500~ms control step} ($11\times$ faster than AIMD).
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\subsection{BDP-Isochronous CoDel/GRED Scaling \& Two-Speed \texttt{kqueue} Cadence}

Finally, to keep internal \texttt{dummynet} GRED marking thresholds isochronous in milliseconds across $r \in [2, 100]\text{ Mbps}$, we scale:
\[
  \mathrm{min\_th}(r) = \mathrm{clamp}_{[2, 24]}(\lfloor r/4 \rfloor + 2), \quad
  \mathrm{max\_th}(r) = \mathrm{clamp}_{[6, 64]}(\lfloor 3r/4 \rfloor + 6), \quad
  \mathrm{slots}(r) = \mathrm{clamp}_{[10, 90]}(r + 10),
\]
