Zero-Bufferbloat Cellular & Wi-Fi Personal Hotspot Optimizer for macOS

Keeps an iPhone, iPad, or Android hotspot responsive under heavy 5G/LTE load — by shaping the queue, not by capping your speed. Runs natively on XNU and reports what it is doing in live state.

In plain terms: it doesn't cap you at a fixed number or guess from pings. It measures upload and download delay separately (no clock sync needed), spots a queue starting to build, and trims the shaper just enough to keep the link responsive — then backs off when it isn't needed. The rules it follows are proved in Lean 4 (110 theorems, zero axioms), so the behaviour is checked rather than tuned by feel.

iPhone / iPad USB High-Speed Tether (en7 / en17)

Detects Apple Mobile Device USB Ethernet interfaces in < 1 ms via AF_ROUTE kernel sockets while strictly excluding USB LAN docks (en9) and VMware passthrough NICs. Keeps awdl0 UP for AirDrop while clamping cellular MTU to 1420 B and TCP MSS to 1380 B.
